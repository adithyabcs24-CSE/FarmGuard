Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

At FarmGuard AI, we respect the privacy of our farmers, agronomists, and partner cooperatives. This Privacy Policy details how we handle imagery, crop data, and account information.

1. Data We Collect

We collect crop and field photographs uploaded for disease analysis, optional location details (such as district and state for regional weather risk alerts), and account contact information.

2. Image Processing & AI Diagnosis

Crop leaf images are processed securely to generate pathology diagnostics and recommendations. We do not sell your field imagery or crop location datasets to commercial seed or commodity trading brokers.

3. Data Security

All passwords are encrypted with bcrypt hashing, and API communication occurs over secure bearer tokens. You can request deletion of your account and scan records at any time.
